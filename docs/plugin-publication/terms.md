## The service

Calorie Counter helps household members record meals, manage calorie goals,
maintain foods and recipes, record cooking batches, and review history. Its
optional assistant integration performs the supported operations using your
connected account's permissions. Features available in the app and through the
assistant differ; the assistant cannot currently delete meals, change goals,
edit recipes, or retrieve stored macronutrient totals.

## Accounts and shared households

Protect your account and approve only connections you initiate. Household access
allows members to view and manage the household's nutrition records, including
records for other tracked people. Share invitation links only with intended
members. Supply information about other people only with appropriate permission.
Revoking an assistant connection does not remove recorded meals or information
already returned to the assistant provider.

## Nutrition and approvals

Nutrition depends on the quantities, ingredients, sources, and assumptions
entered. Model estimates and user-supplied labels are not independent verification
of nutritional accuracy. Review the person, date, portion, and calorie values
before saving. The service is a logging aid and does not supply medical
diagnosis or treatment.

The default assistant policy asks before saving. If you explicitly choose direct
logging, complete non-estimated new meal requests can be previewed and saved in
one turn. Estimates, cooking, corrections, and household transfers still require
review and approval under the integration's instructions. ChatGPT's separate
tool approval controls also apply. These instructions do not create an
independent server-side proof that a human approved each call.

## Your records and acceptable use

You retain rights you have in the information you provide. We process it to
operate the service as described in the privacy policy. Use the service only for
authorized households; do not bypass access controls, misuse another person's
account, or attempt to disrupt the service. Historical references can require
archiving instead of deletion to preserve the meaning of recorded meals.

## Availability and third parties

The service depends on hosted authentication, data, and frontend services, and
on your chosen assistant provider when using that integration. Outages,
connection expiry, usage limits, and model errors can interrupt a request. Check
the recorded result after an uncertain save before requesting another one.
Third-party account terms and privacy settings apply to those services.

## Decisions required before these become terms

- Operator identity and contact, eligibility/age rules, and launch countries.
- Applicable law and any mandatory consumer rights; legally appropriate
  warranties, responsibility limits, dispute process, and termination terms.
- Privacy policy, retention/deletion handling for shared households, and the
  support/export process that the operator can actually deliver.
- Whether there will be charges or paid features; no payment or commerce flow is
  currently implemented by the plugin.
- How users receive and accept terms and material updates, including account
  closure and any relevant notice process.

This draft intentionally leaves those decisions open instead of inventing legal
protections or a service commitment. Finalize it for the chosen operator and
audience before release.
